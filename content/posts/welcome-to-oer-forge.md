Welcome to OER-Forge, a collection of open-source tools built to solve real problems in education.

## Our Mission

We create focused, purpose-built tools that educators can use, modify, and share. Each tool does one thing well and works independently, but together they form a cohesive ecosystem for building and sharing quality educational resources.

## Current Tools

**RaiseMyHand** – Real-time classroom question management. Anonymous student questions ranked by peer upvotes help instructors focus on what matters most.

**WikiAccess** – Convert DokuWiki pages to accessible, WCAG 2.1 compliant documents in multiple formats.
