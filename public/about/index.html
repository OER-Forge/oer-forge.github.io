OER-Forge

Open Educational Resources Tools
1 min · danny caballero

OER-Forge is a collection of tools created to solve real problems in teaching and learning.

Tools#

WikiAccess – Convert DokuWiki pages to accessible, WCAG 2.1 compliant documents. Exports to HTML, Word (.docx), and Markdown. Handles images, LaTeX equations, and generates accessibility reports. Useful for converting course materials into multiple formats while ensuring they’re accessible to everyone. [Repository]

RaiseMyHand – Real-time classroom question management. Students submit questions anonymously and upvote each other’s questions—like Reddit, but for your classroom. Instructors get a dashboard to see, moderate, and respond to the most important questions. Built with FastAPI, WebSockets, and works with Docker. Designed for lectures, labs, seminars, and online courses. [Repository]

jupyter2hugo – A command-line tool to convert Jupyter Notebooks into Hugo-compatible Markdown files. Preserves code cells, outputs, and metadata for seamless integration into Hugo static sites. Ideal for educators and content creators who want to share Jupyter Notebook content on their Hugo-powered websites. [Repository]
