Welcome to OER-Forge, a collection of open-source tools built to solve real problems in education. Our Mission We create focused, purpose-built tools that educators can use, modify, and share. Each tool does one thing well and works independently, but together they form a cohesive ecosystem for building and sharing quality educational resources.
